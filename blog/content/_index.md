**Too Big To Ride** is a VR game project for the course Interaction in Virtual and Augmented Reality. In the duration of 6 months I was tasked to design and implement a parkour game with my own locomotion and interaction techniques. The application runs on Oculus/Meta VR headsets and can be downloaded [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/11faHpWDwuEianEL3CJbLx3M9npXfhlZ5/view?usp=drive_link).

In the game you play as a rabbit that can grow or shrink the world around it. Use the main trigger of both controllers to move and grow/shrink. Use the grab trigger to move and rotate T-shaped objects.
